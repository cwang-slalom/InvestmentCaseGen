# Donor Segments

Use these segments as optional audience lenses. Do not assume any specific
audience unless selected by the user or supported by source context.

## Philanthropic Foundation

Often values catalytic capital, equity, evidence, systems change, and measurable
impact.

## Bilateral or Multilateral Funder

Often values country priorities, implementation feasibility, policy alignment,
and scale.

## Impact Investor

Often values investable vehicles, risk-adjusted return or capital recycling,
market development, and measurable social impact.

## Corporate or Product Partner

Often values product-market fit, distribution, demand generation, and reputation.

## Pooled Fund Contributor

Often values governance, transparency, leverage, and the ability to aggregate
capital around a shared outcome.
