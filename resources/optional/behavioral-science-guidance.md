# Behavioral Science Guidance

Use this guidance when describing adoption, demand, delivery, and stakeholder
behavior.

## Checks

- Identify who must do something differently for the intervention to work.
- Describe barriers to adoption or behavior change using source evidence.
- Avoid assuming that awareness alone will change behavior.
- Surface incentives, trust, access, norms, friction, and timing when relevant.
- Mark behavioral assumptions as assumptions unless supported by source evidence.
