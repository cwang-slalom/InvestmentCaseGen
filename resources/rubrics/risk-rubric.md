# Risk Rubric

Use this rubric to assess risks surfaced in the source material. Do not invent
risks or risk mitigations that are not grounded in the source or clearly marked
as analytical judgment.

## Risk Levels

- Low: Risk is known, bounded, and has credible mitigations.
- Medium: Risk is material but manageable with additional diligence or design.
- High: Risk could prevent implementation, financing, adoption, approval, or impact.
- Unresolved: Source material does not provide enough evidence to assess.

## Risk Categories

- Scientific and technical risk
- Regulatory risk
- Organizational and operational risk
- Commercial and demand risk
- Reputational and stakeholder risk
- Delivery and implementation risk
- Evidence and measurement risk

## Required Output

For each risk category, provide:

- rating
- source evidence
- rationale
- mitigation if supported by evidence
- missing information
