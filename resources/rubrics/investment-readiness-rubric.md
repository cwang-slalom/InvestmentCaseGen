# Investment Readiness Rubric

Use this rubric to assess whether a concept can plausibly become an
investor-facing or donor-facing opportunity.

## Criteria

- Problem clarity
- Intervention clarity
- Evidence strength
- Urgency and timing
- Additionality of capital
- Scale or leverage potential
- Implementability
- Funding recipient or investment vehicle clarity
- Outcome measurability
- Risk transparency
- Fit with likely investor or donor motivations

## Ratings

- Strong: Ready for investment case drafting with limited additional information.
- Moderate: Promising but requires clarification or evidence strengthening.
- Early: Concept may be investable but needs significant definition.
- Not ready: Source material does not support an investment case.

## Required Output

For each assessed concept, provide:

- overall rating
- score by criterion
- evidence supporting the rating
- major gaps
- recommended next diligence questions
