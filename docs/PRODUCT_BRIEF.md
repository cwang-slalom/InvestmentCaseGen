# Product Brief

## Product Name

Investment Case Generator

## Product Goal

Help partnership, philanthropy, and strategy teams identify investable
opportunities within complex strategy documents and transform those
opportunities into compelling, evidence-grounded materials for potential
investors, donors, and funding partners.

The goal is to make the underlying concept attractive and understandable
to potential funders.

The product is not designed to raise money specifically for GPD, the Gates
Foundation, or any single organization.

Depending on the opportunity, funding may be directed to an implementing
organization, nonprofit, pooled fund, government program, research
institution, product developer, multilateral organization, or another
approved investment vehicle.

## Core User Problem

High-potential concepts are often buried in long strategy documents,
technical reports, and internal materials.

Teams must manually:

- identify which concepts are investable
- clarify the intervention and funding need
- identify potential implementing and funding recipients
- assemble supporting evidence
- construct a persuasive narrative
- explain urgency and leverage
- describe impact and risk
- adapt the material for potential investors

This process is slow, inconsistent, and dependent on a small number of
experienced team members.

## MVP Outcome

The MVP should allow a user to upload source documents, identify candidate
investable concepts, select one concept, and generate a source-grounded
investment case draft suitable for human review.

## Product Principles

1. Concept-first, not organization-first.
2. Evidence-grounded, not generic fundraising language.
3. Persuasive but never misleading.
4. Funding recipient must be identified from source evidence or marked
   as unresolved.
5. AI must distinguish between:
   - concept owner
   - implementing organization
   - investment manager
   - funding recipient
   - beneficiary population
6. All outputs require human review.
7. Missing information must be surfaced, not invented.
