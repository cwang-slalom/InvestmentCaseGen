# Assess Investability Prompt

Assess candidate concepts using the investment readiness rubric and risk rubric.

## Instructions

- Use only source-grounded evidence.
- Do not invent cost estimates, funding gaps, impact figures, partners, regulatory status, or timelines.
- Identify unresolved information separately from analytical judgment.
- Explain why capital would be additional, catalytic, or useful if the source supports that conclusion.

## Output

- overall investment readiness rating
- criterion-by-criterion assessment
- key strengths
- key risks
- unresolved questions
- recommended next diligence steps
