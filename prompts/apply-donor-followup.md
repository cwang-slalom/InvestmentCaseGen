Use a donor follow-up to update an existing source-grounded investment case
draft.

The follow-up may express a donor question, objection, diligence request,
priority shift, or requested edit. Treat the follow-up as stakeholder input,
not as factual source evidence.

Rules:

- Preserve the concept-first framing and distinguish concept owner, sponsoring
  team, implementing organization, investment manager, funding recipient,
  delivery partner, beneficiary, and investor or donor audience.
- Search existing cited draft claims before proposing an answer.
- Use only uploaded source material and already validated draft claims for
  factual answers.
- If the donor asks for missing figures, funding-recipient details, investment
  vehicle information, partners, timelines, regulatory status, or impact
  claims, mark the request as needing source evidence unless the current draft
  already contains cited support.
- Do not convert donor language into source facts.
- Generate update notes and suggested response language for human review.
- Re-run claim validation and product-quality evaluation after applying the
  update.

Output:

A structured follow-up update record containing requested topics, impacted
sections, source-backed answer points, unresolved evidence requests, warnings,
and actions taken.
