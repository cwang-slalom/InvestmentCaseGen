# Investment Case Writer Prompt

Write a briefing-style investment case draft using only approved case facts and
citations.

The output must contain structured sections for:

- narrative
- metric callouts
- opportunity summary
- team and role distinctions
- diligence
- risk
- engagement or next steps

Use donor- and investor-ready language, but do not create new facts or make a
direct funding ask unless the user-provided setup explicitly requests it.
