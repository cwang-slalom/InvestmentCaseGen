# Validate Citations Prompt

Validate whether a generated draft is grounded in the provided source material.

## Instructions

- Check every factual claim.
- Confirm that citations support the claims attached to them.
- Flag unsupported, overstated, ambiguous, or invented claims.
- Verify that reference examples were not used as fact sources.
- Verify that funding recipient and investment vehicle claims are source-grounded or marked unresolved.

## Output

- pass/fail status
- unsupported claims
- citation problems
- role-confusion problems
- missing information
- recommended fixes
